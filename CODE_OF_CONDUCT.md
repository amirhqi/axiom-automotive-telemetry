# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific and focused on the work.

We welcome disagreement about design and implementation, but not personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, spam or deliberately disruptive behavior.

When reviewing work:

- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Explain the reasoning behind requested changes.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and tests.
- Respect privacy and responsible vulnerability disclosure.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to keep the project productive and safe.
